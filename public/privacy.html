HR Line — Privacy Policy

App: uz.hrline.employee · Last updated: 29 September 2026

1. What the app does

The HR Line employee app is a mobile shell for the HR Line human-resources platform at hrline.uz. It displays that web service over a secure HTTPS connection. An account issued by your employer is required to use it.

2. What data is collected

The app itself does not independently collect data from your device or share it with third parties. No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs are bundled, and the Advertising ID is not used.

Information you enter or submit in the app (sign-in details, attendance records, requests, location and photos) is sent directly to the hrline.uz server and processed under the terms of the HR Line platform on behalf of your employer, who is the controller of that data.

3. Permissions and why they are needed

  • Internet and network state — to load the web service and detect loss of connectivity.
  • Location (precise and approximate) — to verify your position when you check in or out for attendance. Location is requested only when you perform that action; the app does not track your location in the background.
  • Camera — to take a photo for attendance and to attach photos to records. The camera opens only when you start the relevant feature.

You can revoke any permission at any time in Android settings. Only the features that depend on that permission will stop working.

4. Cookies and sessions

The web service uses cookies and local storage to keep you signed in. This data is stored on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

5. Data deletion

To view, correct or delete your account and related data, contact your employer or the address below.

6. Children

The app is intended for business users and is not directed to children under 13.
